Mic and echo test

Open this page in Browsa, Safari, Arc and Chrome on the same Mac with the same speakers and mic, run the echo test in each, and compare the results table. It plays speech-like sound through your speakers while you stay quiet, and measures how much of it your mic sends back. That leftover is what people on a call hear as echo.

2. Echo test

Set your speakers to a normal call volume, don't wear headphones, and stay quiet while the test sound plays.

Blue bands: test sound playing. Line: what the mic heard, in dBFS. A good result stays flat through the bands.

3. Voice check

Records 5 seconds of you talking, then plays it back, so you can hear how you sound with these settings.
