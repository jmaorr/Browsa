- ⌘T and ⌘K have a new glass look. In ⌘K, press Tab to search the Library and add a tool or extension right there.
- When a link wants to open an app, choose Open, Not Now, Always Open or Don't Open. Change your answer from the site menu or Settings › Privacy.
- More Chrome extensions work, including 1Password and Grammarly. Extensions stay out of private windows unless you allow them in the Library.
- YouTube ads are skipped instead of showing a blank wait, while ad blocking is on.
- Favourites open in the Space you're in, even when Space Routing would send that site elsewhere.
- Phone, FaceTime and Messages links ask before opening, unless you clicked them.
