What’s New

The changes worth knowing about, with a short look at each. The full list for every update is in its release notes.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Split View

Put two or three tabs side by side in one window, and they stay together as one row in the sidebar.

Right-click a tab and choose Add to Split View, or press ⌃⇧=.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Folders for pinned tabs

Group pinned tabs into folders, and folders inside folders. A closed folder still shows the tab you’re on.

Right-click a tab and choose New Folder with Tab, or drag tabs onto a folder.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Live Folders

Your GitHub pull requests and review requests appear in a folder in the sidebar, using the GitHub account you’re already signed in to. Follow a site and its new posts appear in a folder too.

Turn it on in the Library. On a site with a feed, choose Follow in Sidebar from the site menu.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

A profile for each Space

Give a Space its own profile, so Work and Personal keep separate logins. One Gmail favourite can be work mail in one Space and personal mail in another.

Choose a profile for each Space in Settings › Spaces. History, site permissions and extensions are shared by every profile.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Picture in Picture

Leave a tab while a video plays and it pops out into its own window, then goes back when you return. Playing tabs get a play and pause button in the sidebar.

Press ⌥⌘P for any video. Turn off popping out in Settings › General.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Make your own Boosts

Change the font, accent colour and look of any site, or zap the parts you don’t want, in a panel beside the page. Export a Boost to share it.

Find Boosts in the Library, or in the site menu in the address bar.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

Performance

See which tabs are using your Mac’s CPU and memory, and unload a stuck one with a click. Browsa lets you know when a tab in the background is working too hard.

Turn on Performance Monitor in the Library.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft

A new Library

The Library is now one place for Browsa’s tools, packs and extensions, each with a short clip of what it does. ⌘K can search it too.

Open it from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, or press ⌘K then Tab.

Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft
Clip to come

Improvements in 1.3

  • ⌘T and ⌘K have a new glass look. In ⌘K, press Tab to search the Library and add a tool or extension right there.
  • When a link wants to open an app, choose Open, Not Now, Always Open or Don’t Open. Change your answer from the site menu or Settings › Privacy.
  • More Chrome extensions work, including 1Password and Grammarly. Extensions stay out of private windows unless you allow them in the Library.
  • YouTube ads are skipped instead of showing a blank wait, while ad blocking is on.
  • Favourites open in the Space you’re in, even when Space Routing would send that site elsewhere.
  • Phone, FaceTime and Messages links ask before opening, unless you clicked them.
Not yet dated Mac · Version 1.3 Draft
Clip to come

Fixes in 1.3

  • Keyboard shortcuts like ⌘T work again when a page has crashed or stopped responding.
  • Signing in to My Telstra no longer makes the page crash.
  • Answers you give in a private window, like opening an app or using the camera, are no longer saved.
23 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2.1

Site notifications

Sites like Gmail, Slack and Calendar can now send notifications to your Mac while they’re open in a tab.

Turn on notifications from Browsa in Settings › Privacy › Notifications, and change a site’s answer under Site permissions.

23 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2.1

Boosts

Boosts turn on a tidier look for sites you use every day, starting with a LinkedIn feed without the clutter down the side.

Find it in the site icon menu in the address bar, or in Settings › General › Boosts.

23 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2.1

Improvements in 1.2.1

  • Connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or VS Code to Browsa with one Add button in Settings › Agents.
  • Devices on your own network with their own certificates, like Home Assistant or a router, can now be opened with Visit Anyway, and Browsa remembers that certificate. See them in Settings › Privacy › Certificates.
  • Signing in with Google, Apple or Okta opens a small window over the page, and closing it takes you back to the tab you were on.
  • Closing a tab you opened from a link takes you back to the tab it came from, and ⇧⌘T puts a closed tab back where it was.
  • New tabs can open below the current tab instead of at the top, in Settings › General.
  • Archive Today tabs after now has a 48 hours option, in Settings › General.
23 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2.1

Fixes in 1.2.1

  • Links highlight and hover controls appear even when the Browsa window isn’t in front, or after you’ve clicked the call mini-player.
  • Typing on ElevenLabs no longer lags.
  • The call mini-player comes back on screen if the display it was on has been disconnected.
  • Making the sidebar narrow no longer pushes the window buttons out of place.
  • Note pins stay on the page as it scrolls and changes, including on sites with strict security settings.
19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Call mini-player

Leave a call’s tab and the call floats in a small window above everything, with its mute and leave buttons still in reach, and it shrinks to an icon when you need the space.

19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Call badges

Tabs on a call show a camera or microphone badge in the sidebar, and a small capsule over the page takes you back to the call from any other tab.

19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Space themes

Give each space its own colour, lighter or darker, and keep a space light or dark. Browsa itself can now be Light, Dark or follow your Mac.

Right-click a space and choose Colour and Appearance, or go to Settings › Spaces.

19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Favourites in New Tab

⌘T shows your favourites across the top, so you can jump to one even in full screen.

19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Rename tabs

Double-click a tab to give it your own name. Pinned tabs keep it for good.

19 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.2

Fixes in 1.2

  • Pages keep scrolling smoothly after you swipe between Spaces.
  • The zoom level in the address bar updates as you zoom, without hovering over it.
  • Spaces can be dragged into order in Settings, and there are more icons to choose from.
17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Browsa Audio Engine

A new system level audio engine improves echo and noise cancellation in meetings.

You can still choose to use Apple’s WebKit audio in Settings if you prefer.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Space Routing

Sites can open in the Space they belong to, so work tools land in Work and videos in Personal.

Turn it on in Settings › Routing.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Glance

Option-click a link to look at it over the page, then press Esc to put it away or ⌘Return to keep it as a tab.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Bangs

Type !yt, !gh or any of DuckDuckGo’s bangs with a search to go straight to that site’s results.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Calculations

Type a sum or a conversion like 5 km in mi into the address bar or ⌘K and the answer appears as you type.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Tabs for each window

Each window now keeps its own Today tabs, while pinned tabs and favourites stay shared between them.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Call guard

Browsa now checks with you before you close a tab or quit while you’re on a call.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Ask before opening apps

The first time a site tries to open another app, like Zoom or the App Store, Browsa asks and remembers your answer for that site.

Change it any time in Settings › Privacy › Site permissions.

17 November 2026 Mac · Version 1.1

Fixes in 1.1

  • The close, minimise and full-screen buttons now sit in the sidebar’s top row and travel with it.
  • Settings has a clearer layout, with each section on its own page.
  • In ⌘K, the arrow keys start from the highlighted row and reach the row of tools.
  • Extensions' toolbar buttons open their side panels, and FireShot saves its captures.
  • Private windows don’t keep zoom levels, permission answers or site icons.
  • Tabs on a call, playing sound or holding something you’ve typed are no longer unloaded to save memory.
  • Apps and installers you download get macOS’s usual safety check the first time you open them.
  • A new window opens at the size of the window you were using, instead of small.
  • In full screen, the sidebar stays put when your pointer rests at the screen edge or one of its menus is open.
  • One swipe on the sidebar moves one Space.
  • Opening a link while Browsa is closed now opens it in a new window.
  • More breathing room around the sidebar’s top row.
  • The loading bar follows the page’s rounded corners.
