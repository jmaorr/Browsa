Your browsing isn't our business.

No account and no analytics. Your tabs and history stay on your device, and Browsa blocks ads, trackers and cookie banners.

The privacy policy lists every service it talks to, like your search engine, and what each one receives.

Open source, so you can check.

Browsa's code is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it, build it, or fork it into your own browser.
