Make it yours, a switch at a time.

The Library holds everything you can add to Browsa: our own plugins, in two packs, and Chrome extensions we’ve tested. Open it from ⌘K.

Agent Pack

Radar finds your dev servers, Notes holds your feedback on pages, and Agents lets Claude Code, Codex or Cursor work through Browsa while you watch.

Privacy Pack

On from the start. Ads and trackers are blocked, cookie banners are declined for you, and tracking comes off links before pages load.

Chrome extensions, verified to work

1PasswordPasswords, passkeys and cards, filled for you.
uBlock Origin LiteBlocks ads and trackers, with no setup.

VimiumBrowse with the keyboard: press f to click any link.
JSON FormatterReadable, collapsible JSON in the browser.
Paper SnapshotCopy any part of a site into Paper as editable layers.
Google TranslateSelect text on any page to translate it.

Add any of these in one click. Most others from the Chrome Web Store run too, through a compatibility layer on Apple’s WebKit; the ones above are the ones we’ve tested. Names and icons belong to their owners.

A browser your agents can share.

Switch on the Agent Pack and add Browsa to Claude Code in one click. Codex, Cursor and other MCP agents get a line to paste. Then they can open pages, read them and test your work, in the browser you’re already signed in to.

  • In their own space. Agents work in an Agent space. Your window never switches to it unless you choose to watch.
  • Your tabs stay yours. In its own space an agent never stops to ask. Reading or using one of your tabs, or even seeing their list, asks you first.
  • Notes for both of you. Leave a note on a page and your agent reads it, replies and resolves it.
  • Stop, always in reach. At the top of the sidebar while an agent drives, and in the Agents panel.
