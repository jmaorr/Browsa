Browsa, now on your Mac.

A calm, keyboard-first browser with a sidebar for your spaces, a panel for your tools, and room for the extensions and agents you already use. Native WebKit, open source, and free.

Bring the extensions you rely on.

Browsa installs extensions from where they already live and keeps them up to date itself, once a day. If an update wants more access, it asks you first.

A browser your agents can share.

Turn on Agents in Settings and coding agents like Claude Code can open pages, read them and test your work, in the browser you're already signed in to.

  • In their own space. Agents open pages in an Agent space, outlined so you always know.
  • Asked, not assumed. Acting on a new site asks you first. Your own localhost never does.
  • One Stop button. It halts anything that would change a page, until you say so.
