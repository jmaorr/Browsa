Background audio and Picture in Picture

A test page for any browser on iPhone and iPad. Play something, leave the browser, and see what keeps going.

1. Background audio

  1. Tap play.
  2. Go to the Home Screen, or open another app.
  3. Listen: the music should keep playing, with controls in Control Centre and on the Lock Screen.
  4. Come back here to see how long it played while you were away.

2. Picture in Picture

  1. Play the video.
  2. Tap the Picture in Picture button in the player, or go fullscreen and swipe up to the Home Screen.
  3. The video should float over the Home Screen and other apps, with sound. The clock keeps counting while it plays.

Why some sites stop anyway

Some sites, YouTube among them, pause their own playback when the page is hidden. They do it in every browser, Safari included, so they can't tell you what the browser supports. This page never pauses itself, so what you hear is down to the browser alone.

About the media

The music and the video were made for this page. They're free to use for testing.
