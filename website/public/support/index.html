Common questions

How do I open a private tab?

Press and hold any plus button, then choose New Private Tab. The Private space goes away, with its cookies and storage, when its last tab closes.

How do I get back to a recent tab?

Press and hold the tabs button, or the sidebar button where the sidebar is your tab list.

A site looks broken. What can I try?

Turn off blockers for that site from its page menu, then reload.

How do I sign out of sites everywhere?

Open Settings and choose Clear Cookies and Site Data.
