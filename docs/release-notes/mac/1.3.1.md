**New: Screenshots**
Take a picture of the page you're on. It's saved to your Downloads folder and appears in Downloads beside the page.
*Choose View › Take Screenshot, or type Screenshot in ⌘K.*

**New: Export Spaces**
Save your Favourites and every Space as a bookmarks file that any browser can import.
*Find it in Settings › Spaces.*

**Improvements**
- ⌃Tab goes back to the tab you used last. Keep Control down and press Tab again to go further back. To move down the sidebar as before, turn it off in Settings › General.
- Downloads opens beside the page when a download finishes, and you can drag a finished file out, onto a page or into another app. Turn off the opening in Settings › General.
- A video can stay in its Space when you switch to another, instead of popping out. Turn off Also when you switch Space in Settings › General.
- Comment on a page with ⌥C. It opens Notes if needed, Escape leaves it from anywhere, and a Stay switch lets you add one note after another.

**Fixes**
- A blank box that couldn't be clicked no longer appears over your screen while an agent uses Browsa.
- Download Image, Download Linked File and Download Video in a page's right-click menu now download.
- The setting for local sites with certificate errors now lists every private address range it covers.
