**New: Site notifications**
Sites like Gmail, Slack and Calendar can now send notifications to your Mac while they're open in a tab.
*Turn on notifications from Browsa in Settings › Privacy › Notifications, and change a site's answer under Site permissions.*

**New: Site Styles**
Turn on a tidier look for sites you use every day, starting with a LinkedIn feed without the clutter down the side.
*Find it in the site icon menu in the address bar, or in Settings › General › Site styles.*

**Improvements**
- Connect Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or VS Code to Browsa with one Add button in Settings › Agents.
- Devices on your own network with their own certificates, like Home Assistant or a router, can now be opened with Visit Anyway, and Browsa remembers that certificate. See them in Settings › Privacy › Certificates.
- Signing in with Google, Apple or Okta opens a small window over the page, and closing it takes you back to the tab you were on.
- Closing a tab you opened from a link takes you back to the tab it came from, and ⇧⌘T puts a closed tab back where it was.
- New tabs can open below the current tab instead of at the top, in Settings › General.
- Archive Today tabs after now has a 48 hours option, in Settings › General.

**Fixes**
- Links highlight and hover controls appear even when the Browsa window isn't in front, or after you've clicked the call mini-player.
- Typing on ElevenLabs no longer lags.
- The call mini-player comes back on screen if the display it was on has been disconnected.
- Making the sidebar narrow no longer pushes the window buttons out of place.
- Note pins stay on the page as it scrolls and changes, including on sites with strict security settings.
